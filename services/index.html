Manufacturing Excellence

Precision Industrial Components.

From gear couplings and cutting tools to wearing parts and heavy equipment fabrication — manufactured in Davao City with micron-level precision for industries across Mindanao.

01 · Power Transmission

Power Transmission & Drive Components

Engineered for high-torque applications, our gear couplings provide superior compensation for axial, radial, and angular misalignment in heavy rotating machinery.

Types
Shafts & hubs
Spline shafts, axles, eccentric shafts, motor shafts, taper locks
Gears & Sprockets
Spur gears, helical gears, worm gears and worm shafts, bevel gears, spiral bevel gears, herringbone gears, sprockets
Pulleys & Couplings
Timing pulley, flange couplings, gear couplings, jaw couplings, oldham couplings
02 · Material Processing

Cutting, Forming & Industrial Tooling

Industrial-grade cutting solutions designed for longevity and surgical precision. Our blades utilize custom heat-treatment processes to ensure edge retention in the toughest environments.

Types
Blade, slotters & cutters
Slotter blades, industrial knives, die cutters
Punchers & Dies
Concave punchers, serrated punchers, punching dies, die core
Blanks and seamers
Seaming chucks, seaming rolls, blanking holders
03 · Kinematics

Wearing & Support Components

From spur and helical to complex worm gears, we manufacture the movement that powers your innovation. Every gear is tested for noise reduction and load-bearing efficiency.

Types
Bolts & Nuts
Standard and custom threaded fasteners.
Pins & Bushings
Dowel pins, clevis pins, or taper pins for alignment and pivoting
Fittings & Nozzles
Tips and connectors for piping, hydraulic or pneumatic systems
Sleeves
Protective outer layers for shafts or specialized mechanical spacers
04 · Heavy Equipment

Industrial and Heavy Equipment Component Fabrication

End-to-end fabrication of critical components for heavy industrial equipment. From rotating assemblies to structural support, every part is engineered for durability under demanding operational conditions.

Types
Rotating Components
Impellers, fans, agitator blades
Support Components
Frames and pedestals
Transport Components
Chain wheel, conveyors, gearboxes
Drive Components
Head wheels & drive shafts

Precision Machining and Grinding
