About Us.

Rooted in Davao, serving all of Mindanao. We define the gold standard in industrial craftsmanship for over five decades.

The Heritage

A Legacy Forged in Steel.

Founded in 1969 amidst the burgeoning industrial landscape of Davao City, Deco Machine Shop, Inc. began with a singular vision: to provide local industries with the mechanical precision required for national progress.

What started as a small-scale workshop has evolved into a premier industrial hub. For more than 50 years, we have remained anchored to our Davao roots while expanding our capabilities to serve the entire Mindanao region.
